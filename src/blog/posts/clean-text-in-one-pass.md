Most text editing is not writing. It is fixing the shape of text that was already written: pasting a list and getting doubled lines, turning a title into a URL, or cleaning capitals after a paste from a PDF. Five small tools cover those chores.

## Case conversion

Copied text arrives in whatever case the source used. A headline needs title case, a shouting comment needs lower case, and a description often reads best in sentence case. Converting a whole block at once avoids retyping and the typos that come with it.

One caveat: title case rules differ by style guide. The converter capitalises each word, which is fine for headings and labels; for article titles, check small words like "of" and "the" by eye.

## Line-break removal

Text copied from a PDF or a narrow column arrives with a hard break every few words. Joined back into paragraphs, it becomes normal text again. The reverse job - splitting one long line into separate lines - is useful for building lists and keyword columns.

## Duplicate lines

Mailing lists, keyword exports and merged spreadsheets collect repeated lines. Removing duplicates while keeping the original order is the standard fix. A quick review after the dedupe is still worth it: two lines that look identical can differ by a trailing space.

## URL slugs

A clean slug - lowercase words separated by hyphens - is easier to share and to read in search results. The [Text Tools page](/text-tools/) also converts accented letters to their closest Latin form and replaces every other symbol with a hyphen, so "Café & Crème!" becomes `cafe-creme`. That matches how most sites normalise URLs.

Keep slugs short and stable. Changing one after publishing breaks links and loses whatever ranking the old URL earned.

## Placeholder text

Design mockups need realistic-looking text of the right length. Lorem ipsum keeps reviewers focused on layout instead of reading the copy, and a paragraph-count control is faster than hunting for a sample block.

## One honest limit

These tools work on the text in the page, not on files. Nothing is stored, nothing is uploaded, and closing the tab clears it. Keep the original document until the cleaned version is pasted back and checked.
