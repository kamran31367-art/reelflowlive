A thumbnail is the first thing a viewer sees, and the wrong dimensions make it look soft or leave black bars on the sides. YouTube is clear about what it accepts, so there is no reason to guess.

## The numbers that matter

- **Recommended size:** 1280 x 720 pixels.
- **Minimum width:** 640 pixels.
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9, the same shape as the video player.
- **Maximum file size:** 2 MB.
- **Accepted formats:** JPG, PNG and GIF.

A 1280 x 720 JPG is the safest choice for almost every upload. It is large enough to stay sharp on a TV, small enough to upload quickly, and every editor can export it.

## Why other sizes cause problems

If the image is not 16:9, YouTube has to crop or pad it. Text near the edges can be cut off, and thin borders can disappear. A square image from Instagram usually loses its top and bottom when it becomes a thumbnail.

If the file is under 640 pixels wide, YouTube may still accept it, but it will look blurry on a large screen. Scaling a small image up never adds detail.

## Keep the important part in the middle

The player shows a small version of the thumbnail in search results, on channel pages and on phones. Fine details vanish at that size.

- Use one clear subject or one short phrase.
- Keep text at least the height of about one tenth of the image.
- Leave a margin around the edges so cropping is harmless.
- Check the thumbnail at phone size before you publish.

A simple test is to shrink the image to 320 pixels wide. If the idea still reads, it will work everywhere.

## Shorts and vertical covers

Shorts are vertical, so a 16:9 thumbnail is not a good fit for anything shown in a vertical frame. If you need a cover for a Short or a vertical post on another platform, start from **1080 x 1920** and keep the subject in the middle third.

You can still use 1280 x 720 for the regular video thumbnail that appears in search and on the watch page.

## Quick checklist

- 1280 x 720 pixels, 16:9.
- JPG or PNG, under 2 MB.
- No important text near the edges.
- Readable at 320 pixels wide.
- Vertical cover at 1080 x 1920 when the platform is vertical.
