A thumbnail can look perfect in the editor and lose its punch in the feed. Platforms crop the same 16:9 image into different shapes, draw gradients and badges over it, and show it at sizes you never see while designing. Safe zones are the fix: a map of what survives.

## What covers the image

Four things happen on top of a YouTube thumbnail:

- **The player frame** - a straight 16:9 crop, the full design.
- **A dark gradient and the duration badge** - drawn over the bottom edge, with the badge in the bottom-right corner.
- **The search card crop** - tighter than the player, losing a slice from the sides.
- **The phone feed crop** - closer to a vertical square, which cuts both sides hard.

Text near the right edge disappears under the duration badge. Text near the sides disappears in the phone feed. Text at the very bottom sits in the gradient and loses contrast.

## The safe area in practice

- Keep every essential word inside the **central 80 percent** of the frame.
- Treat the bottom-right corner as occupied.
- Put faces and logos in the middle band, not in the corners.
- Leave the very edges for background that can be cropped without meaning.

## Test before publishing

The [Thumbnail Safe-Zone Tester](/thumbnail-safe-zone/) overlays each of those crops on your open thumbnail so you can switch between them and see exactly which words survive. You can also export a copy with the guides drawn on it to compare against the final upload.

## Design for the smallest view

The search card on a phone is the smallest common presentation, and it is where a busy design collapses. A thumbnail that still reads at that size - one main subject, three or four words, high contrast - usually holds up everywhere else.

## A quick checklist

- Key words centred, never hugging an edge.
- Nothing important under the bottom-right badge.
- Dark text on light boxes or light text with an outline, not mid-grey on mid-grey.
- The design still works when the sides are cut.
- The image is checked in the safe-zone view, not only at full size in the editor.
